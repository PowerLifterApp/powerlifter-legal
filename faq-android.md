# Powerlifter: questions and answers

Powerlifter is a training log for conjugate and Westside-style powerlifting on Android. This page answers the questions people ask most.

---

## Your first workout

### Your first workout, step by step

1. Tap **+** on Home.
2. In **Add Exercise**, search by name (Box squat, Bench press, Deadlift) and tap the lift.
3. Type the weight on the bar and the reps.
4. Tap the circle (○) near the end of the row. That logs the set as made. Missed it? Tap the ✗ beside the circle instead.
5. Add the next lift with **Add Exercise** at the bottom.
6. Tap **Finish** at the top.
7. The workout is on Home now, under the calendar. Tap it any time to read or change it.

That's the whole app on most days. The rest is optional.

### Five things worth knowing on day one

1. **An exercise is what the lift is; a setup is how it's loaded** (bar, bands, chains). You log a setup.
2. **Type the bar plus plates, never the band tension.** The bands belong to the setup.
3. **The ⋯ on a lift** holds everything else: change the setup, bar or bands, **Edit all sets**, delete the lift.
4. **The gear at the top left** (its small square shows KG or LB) holds the unit switch, **Appearance**, **Data controls…** and **Help**.
5. **Home is your whole history.** Scroll it, or search it by lift or date.

## Getting started

### Where are the settings?

Tap the **gear** at the top left of Home, Progress or Library; its small square shows your unit, **KG** or **LB**. It has the unit switch, **Appearance**, **Data controls…** and **Help**.

### How do I change the look?

Open **Appearance** from the gear at the top left.

- **Look:** System, Light or Dark. System follows your phone.
- **Style:** Standard, Iron or Clipboard. Iron is steel and hard edges. Clipboard is a paper notebook with pen marks.
- **Logger:** Classic or Dock. Dock puts the big load and the plate cartoon up top, with **Made** and **Miss** within thumb reach.
- **Timer:** Ring, Scoreboard or Pad. Scoreboard is a square LED dial with a progress bar. Pad is a paper stopwatch face.
- **App icon:** Bend, Bands, Chain, Glass, Glyph or Rack. Android changes the launcher icon without asking.

Both Looks work with every Style, and every Style, logger, timer face and icon is free.

### Where is help?

Tap the **gear** at the top left of Home, Progress or Library, then **Help**. It opens this list, with a search field at the top.

### How do I switch between kilograms and pounds?

Tap the **gear** at the top left and choose **Change to pounds** or **Change to kilograms**. Everything is stored exactly, so switching never changes your numbers.

### Can one lift use kilograms while the rest of the app uses pounds?

Yes, in two ways:

- **Every time you do an exercise:** in **Library**, open the exercise and set **Default unit** to **Kilograms (kg)** or **Pounds (lbs)**. New lifts of that exercise start in that unit. **App default** follows the unit switch in the gear menu.
- **Just today's lift:** in a workout, tap ⋯ on the lift and choose **Use kilograms** or **Use pounds**. Only that lift changes.

Handy for kettlebells or a competition bar loaded in kilos while your gym's plates are in pounds.

### Can I bring my history over from another app?

Yes, with an AI assistant doing the conversion. There's no direct import from other apps. Go to **Data controls → Backup, import & export → From another app (AI chat)**:

1. Export your history from the other app. A CSV, a spreadsheet or notes all work.
2. Tap **Copy prompt**, then paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini along with your file.
3. Save what it returns as a `.json` file, then import it with **Import a Powerlifter file…**.

Check a few sessions after importing. Importing the same file again skips the days it already added, but days you logged by hand in Powerlifter are not skipped, so import old history before you start logging, or into days you haven't used. The assistant may ask you a question first (a unit, a date format); answer it rather than let it guess.

## Logging a workout

### How do I start a workout?

Tap **+** on Home. The logger opens on a new workout dated now. To log a past day, select that day on the calendar first, then tap **+**.

If your coach writes the plan on paper or a whiteboard, tap the **camera** on Home instead and take a photo of it. Powerlifter reads the sheet and fills in the workout for you to check (see the question about starting a workout from a photo).

### How do I add another lift to this workout?

At the bottom of the workout, tap **Add Exercise** and pick the lift. It is added to the workout you already have open.

### How do I log a set?

Type the weight and reps in the entry row, then tap the circle (○) near the end of the row to log the set as made. Tap the ✗ beside it to log a miss instead. A made set shows a check. Tap that check to mark the set a miss, with a reason if you want one. With the Dock logger, tap **Made** or **Miss** instead. Planned sets are checked off one at a time, top to bottom. Made, Miss and delete each offer **Undo** for a few seconds.

### How do I change or delete a set?

- Tap a logged set to open **Edit set** and change it.
- Swipe a set away to delete it. **Undo** is offered for a few seconds.
- To make a logged set a warm-up, or a working set again, open **Edit set** and use the **Warm-up** switch.

To change reps or weight on every set of the lift, tap ⋯ on the lift and choose **Edit all sets…**. Pick the **Reps** and **Weight** change, choose what **Applies to**, turn on **Include warm-ups** if you want them changed, and tap **Apply**. Logged sets ask you to confirm with **Change**. Reps and weights on logged sets are replaced, and you can undo right after.

### What weight do I type: the bar and plates, or the total with bands?

Type the **bar plus plates**: the free weight. Bands and chains are part of the setup, so Powerlifter adds them for you where they matter. Your 1RMs and records always count the free weight only.

### How do I log a specialty bar with bands?

Add the bar in **Library → Gym → Bars**. Adding a bar makes setups for the main lifts that bar is used for. Another lift gets a setup the first time you log it on that bar.

Start the workout and tap **Add Exercise**, or **Add Exercise** at the bottom of a workout you already opened. Pick the lift on that bar, such as box squat on the safety squat bar. If the bands are not already part of the setup, tap ⋯ on the lift and choose **Change bands / chains…**. Type the **bar plus plates**. Powerlifter adds the band tension.

### How do I log a bodyweight lift like pull-ups?

Log only the weight you add (or take off with assistance). The lift shows it as **BW+20**, for example. Your body weight comes from the number you saved in **Data controls → Body weight & Health Connect**.

### How do I finish a workout?

Tap **Finish** in the logger. If you close the logger instead, the workout stays in progress and Home shows a card with **Open** and **Finish**. A short workout asks whether to save or discard it.

### How do I find an old workout?

Home is the calendar plus your history. Scroll down or search it by lift name or date. While you type, the calendar steps aside so the results sit above the keyboard, and the search looks at every day, whichever day is selected. Clear the search and the calendar comes back where you left it.

### How do I see what I did last time on this lift?

Tap **Previous lifts** on the lift (or **Prev** in the Dock layout). You can filter to **All**, **Work** or **1RM**, and copy a past day into this workout with **Copy to this workout**.

### How do I repeat a whole workout?

On Home, press and hold the workout and tap **Copy as new…**. Pick the date and tap **Create**. The new workout has the same lifts. **Copy set structure (as planned)** and **Include weights** are off until you turn them on. Notes and feelings start fresh. A meet is copied without the Competition mark, so tap that again if the new day is a meet.

### Can I plan a workout ahead of time?

Yes. On Home, press and hold a past workout and tap **Copy as new…**. Pick a later day and tap **Plan**. For today, turn on **Plan it**. From a photo of your sheet, tap **Plan for…** instead of **Start workout** and pick the day. Or select a later day on the calendar and tap **+**, or hold today's date and choose **Plan for later**. A plan's day shows a hollow dot on the calendar. A plan stays out of your history, the calendar's dots, Progress and Review until you start it. Open it from its day on the calendar to change lifts or loads. On the day, Home shows it with **Open** and **Start**. **Start** starts the workout now, and logging its first set starts it too. A plan lifted on another day is dated the day you lift it. A plan you didn't start stays on its day as **Planned · missed**: press and hold it to **Move to today** or **Delete**.

### How do I delete a workout?

On Home, press and hold the workout and tap **Delete**, then confirm. Or open the workout, tap **Session** at the top, and tap **Delete workout**. It asks you to confirm. Every lift and set in that workout is removed.

### How do I log a circuit?

At the bottom of the workout, tap **Add circuit**. Pick the lifts and the rounds. With no clock, tap **Apply**. With a clock, the button reads **Apply…**, and you choose **Apply** or **Apply & start timer**. On the workout, **Finish round** checks off the round you are on. You can also check each lift yourself.

### Can I start a workout from a photo of my written plan?

Yes. Tap the camera on Home and take or pick a photo of the sheet. Text recognition runs on your phone; the picture is never sent to us. You review every line before it becomes a workout. This is a beta feature.

### How do I log an injury?

Hold **+** on Home, or hold a date on the calendar, and choose **Injury**. Type what hurts (for example "Left shoulder"), pick the day it started, and save. It's a note in your journal: it doesn't change your workouts or hide any exercise.

You don't have to say when an injury is over. **Still going** stays on until you end it, and nothing nags you. When you're ready, hold a date (or **+**) inside the injury and choose **End** with its name, or tap the injury's row and turn **Still going** off. Turning it back on reopens it if it flares up. Injuries are for your own record, not medical advice.

### How do I mark a treatment day, a vacation, or a sick day?

Hold **+** on Home, or hold a date on the calendar, and choose **Treatment**, **Vacation** or **Sick**. A future day offers those three only. You can train on the same day.

### How do I mark a meet?

Open the workout and tap **Session** at the top. Under **Workout type**, tap **Competition**. A weigh-in row appears for the meet body weight.

### Where is Review?

On **Progress**, tap **Review** at the top right.

## Exercises, setups and groups

### What's the difference between an exercise and a setup?

- An **exercise** is what the lift *is*: Box squat, 2" deficit box squat, Floor press, Sumo deadlift. A height, pin, board count or stance makes a different exercise, because you'd look up its max separately.
- A **setup** is how the exercise is loaded that day: which bar, which bands or chains, and any extra load. "Box squat · Safety squat bar · #2 bands" is a setup.

A test that settles most cases: **would you look up its max on its own next time?** If yes, it's an exercise. Assisted is part of the setup: a pull-up with help is the Pull-up exercise on an assisted setup, so your history stays in one place. On an assisted setup the number you type is the help you used, and less help is better.

You log a setup. Progress can show your numbers by exercise or by the exact setup.

### What are groups?

Groups are folders for browsing the library and filtering Progress, like "Squat" or "Bench". An exercise can be in more than one group. Groups don't change how anything is logged.

### How do I add an exercise the library doesn't have?

Open **Library**, then **Exercises**, and tap **+** (**Add exercise**). Type the name and tap **Save**. A height, pin, board or stance that you would max on its own is its own exercise.

### How do I add bands or chains to a lift?

Make a setup with them: **Library → Setups → +**, pick the exercise and bar, then add the bands or chains. You can also copy an existing setup (press and hold it, then **Copy**) and change only the bands. During a workout, tap ⋯ on the lift and choose **Change bands / chains…**.

### How do I switch the setup on a lift I've started?

Tap ⋯ on the lift and choose:

- **Change setup…** picks a different saved setup. If sets are already logged, **Start a new lift** keeps them on this lift and puts the new setup underneath with the planned sets. **Rewrite this lift** moves every set onto the new setup. A lift in a circuit only offers rewrite.
- **Edit setup…** changes this setup's name, its bar, and **Extra load**.

Bands and chains stay on **Change bands / chains…**.

### I added a bar to my gym. Where are its setups?

Adding a bar in **Library → Gym → Bars** creates setups for the main lifts that bar is used for. Other lifts get a setup the first time you log them on it.

### Why can't I delete an exercise, bar or band?

Anything a logged workout uses can't be deleted, so your history never loses its parts. You can rename it. One you've never logged can be deleted: in **Library**, swipe it left and tap **Delete**.

### I changed a bar's weight (or a setup's bands). Does my old history change?

To change a bar, tap it in **Library → Gym → Bars**. When that would change numbers you've already logged, Save asks. **Keep logged totals** leaves every logged set as it is and only updates the bar. The other button moves every logged set on that bar by the difference. Cancel leaves the sheet open. A bar nothing has been logged on saves without asking.

## Bands and chains

### How does Powerlifter know how much my bands add?

You tell it, once. Bands pull differently on each lift, so each pair of bands has a **lockout** number for each lift (squat, bench, deadlift, rack pull, shoulder press). Enter them in **Library → Gym → Paired bands → Lockouts…**. Chains are entered by their weight.

Powerlifter never guesses. If a lockout is blank, the setup shows **Not set** and nothing is added.

### How do I measure my band tension?

Bands pull differently for every setup, so measure yours at lockout and at the bottom, with a scale or a load cell, and enter what you get. Powerlifter never guesses.

### What's the "bottom" number for bands?

The band tension at the bottom of the lift. It counts toward that day's volume. Set it with the **Top | Bottom** switch on the Lockouts page. A blank bottom counts as zero.

### My bands are set up differently at another rack. How do I record that?

Use an **anchor**: a second column of lockout numbers for the same bands on the same lift, for example "Spotter arms" or a specific rack. A new lift starts on the anchor you used last time on that setup.

### Do bands count in my 1RM?

No. Your 1RMs and records are the free weight on the bar. On **Progress → 1RM**, a chip lets the chart blend in a share of the band and chain tension.

### Can I see my band tension as a percent of my max?

Yes. When you add bands or chains to a setup, the screen shows the lockout total and what percent of that lift's 1RM it is (for example "70 lb · 14%"). The 1RM is your heaviest made single on that exercise in the last 12 months, bar and plates only.

## Max-effort days and 1RMs

### How do I build a max-effort ladder?

In a workout, tap **1RM** on the lift. Enter the target and Powerlifter plans the warm-ups and singles from the empty bar up (a deadlift starts with plates on the bar), with even jumps and the last two about 5% apart. You can edit any row before you apply it. Turn on **Lazy plating** on that screen to avoid plates under 5 lb (2.5 kg) a side where the rules allow.

### What are the suggested numbers in the 1RM planner?

Estimates from your own past max-effort days: first the same exercise on other setups, then related lifts. A partial lift (like a rack pull) never feeds a full one. They're suggestions; you pick the target.

### Where do I see my max-effort history?

**Progress → 1RM** lists every max-effort day as a ladder, with a chart. A day counts when it has five or more singles that climb. One heavy single does not. It is still a PR when you tap Made, and your best squat, bench and deadlift are on **Progress → Benchmarks**. Rows with other than seven singles show **short** or **over**. You can star a plan that worked.

### How do I load 40% of my 1RM quickly?

On the lift's next empty work set, tap **Fill** and pick a percent from 25 to 90, in 5% steps. The chip reads Fill on every row, and with no past loads the panel holds only the percent strip. The pick fills that set with that share of your max, rounded to your plates, and shows a line like "40% of 405 lb · Box squat" so you can see where it came from. To plan a whole template at one percent, like 8×8, open **Set template** from the lift and tap **Fill** beside **Weight**. It is the same panel.

The max is your heaviest made single on that exercise in the last 12 months, bar and plates only. If the exercise has none, it uses the main competition lift (squat, bench or deadlift) and names it. The percents show only on barbell lifts that have a max to use. Bands and chains aren't counted.

### Where are my all-time records and my total?

**Progress → Benchmarks.** The **Total** at the top shows your best squat, bench and deadlift and your total. These are your all-time bests. Below it are the lifts you've chosen to track and your other tests.

### Why didn't my heavy single show as a PR?

A PR is the most free weight you've ever made on that setup or exercise. Bands, chains and extra load don't count, and the first time you log a lift there's nothing to beat yet. On an assisted lift (an assisted pull-up, the inverse curl), less assistance is the PR. PRs are judged when you tap Made during a workout in progress. You can turn the celebration off in **Data controls → Training → Celebrate PRs**.

## Timers

### How do I start a timer?

Tap **Tmpl** on a lift to open the **Set template** sheet. Pick a timer, or make one with **New interval…**. **Apply** keeps the timer on the lift, and **Apply & start** also starts it. A timer on a lift shows its own start button and an **Edit** button.

### Does the timer check off my sets?

The Clock and W/R faces check off a planned set each interval when they're attached to a lift. Hold logs each finished hold on a timed lift. Marks never touch your sets.

### Does the timer keep going when my phone is locked?

The timer runs on the clock, so its time stays right if you lock the phone or switch apps. On a locked phone you get a short notification at each change, if you've allowed notifications. Turn on **Alarms & reminders** for Powerlifter in Android's settings so those notifications arrive on time. If Android closes the app, the timer ends, so keep Powerlifter open while you train.

## Body weight and Health Connect

### What does Powerlifter read from Health Connect?

Only with your permission, and only your **body weight**: to suggest your body weight, to show a day's weight on Home and to chart your history on Progress. Powerlifter reads nothing else from Health Connect.

### What does Powerlifter write to Health Connect?

One thing, and only if you allow it: **a body weight you type**. A weigh-in or **Body weight & Health Connect** saves it to Health Connect for that day. **Remove this weigh-in** deletes it. A number that came from Health Connect is never written back, and weights from your scale or other apps are never changed.

### I said no to Health Connect. How do I turn it on?

In your phone's settings open **Health Connect → App permissions → Powerlifter** and allow body weight. Then use **Read Health Connect** in **Data controls → Body weight & Health Connect**. If Health Connect is installed, **Open Health Connect** on that page takes you there.

## Backups and your data

### Can I export my data?

Yes. **Data controls → Backup, import & export**:

- **Export backup…** saves a full backup you can import again.
- **Export history spreadsheet…** saves a CSV with one row per set.

The file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

### How do I restore a backup?

**Data controls → Backup, import & export → Import a Powerlifter file…** and pick a Powerlifter backup file. A backup skips days already on this phone, so importing twice doesn't duplicate anything.

### How do I delete my data?

In **Data controls → Backup, import & export**:

- **Delete all session data…** removes every workout, set, max-effort plan and sheet photo. Your library (exercises, bars, gym) stays.
- **Factory reset…** also clears your library and puts the defaults back.

Neither can be undone, so export a backup first if you might want it.

### Do I need an account?

No. There's no Powerlifter account and no Powerlifter server.

## Privacy

### What data do you collect?

None. There are no ads, analytics or tracking, and no Powerlifter server.

### Is Powerlifter medical or coaching advice?

No. It's a log, not a coach or a medical device. You're responsible for your own programming and safety. For training or injury questions, ask your coach or a doctor.
